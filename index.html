Two tracks, one center

Which one is yours?

Every CLIMB venture runs on the same four-phase framework — Discovery, Design, Test, Evolve — but who you're building for changes everything else. Pick the track that matches what you're building.

MGNT 291 / 491

Forge

Student-owned ventures. You define the need, the market, and the model — and you keep the reward.

DISCOVERY · DESIGN · TEST · EVOLVE
  • For founders solving a problem they intend to own
  • Mostly solo — co-found with at most one partner
  • Works with or without MGNT 250, at your own pace
  • Four tools, four gates, one lead mentor
MGNT 288 / 488

Impact

Community-owned projects. The community defines the need; your team serves it — and the impact is what stays.

DISCOVERY · DESIGN · TEST · EVOLVE
  • For teams serving a problem that belongs to a community
  • Team-based, instructor-scored gate reviews
  • Built around MGNT 288/488, the Community Ventures Lab
  • Four tools, four gates, sustainability built in
